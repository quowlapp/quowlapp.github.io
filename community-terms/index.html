Community Terms

The rules for sharing your own quotes and facts with the Quowl Community.

Effective November 20, 2026

These terms apply when you share your own quotes or facts with the Quowl Community ("Community"). They are in addition to the Apple standard Terms of Use for the app and to our Privacy Policy.

1. What you can submit

You can submit short quotes and facts. A quote must be your own words, or the words of a person you name as the author. A fact must be something you believe to be true and, where you can, a source for it. Submissions are limited in length: up to 500 characters in the app, and longer items (a quote over 200 or a fact over 140 characters) are not published.

2. Your responsibility

You are responsible for what you submit. In particular you confirm that:

  • the text is your own, or you have named its real author correctly;
  • you do not copy long passages from books, songs, articles or other copyrighted works;
  • it does not contain personal data of other people (names with contact details, addresses, phone numbers) or links and advertising.

3. What is not allowed

Hateful, harassing, discriminatory, sexually explicit, violent, self-harm, illegal or deceptive content, impersonation, spam, and content that infringes someone else's rights. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and for abuse of the Community.

4. Review and removal

Every submission is reviewed before anything appears in the app. We may reject a submission, or remove a published item, at any time and without notice. Other users can report any Community item with the report button; we act on reports promptly, and objectionable items and users who abuse the Community are removed.

5. License you give us

By submitting, you give Quowl a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to store, format and show your text (and the author or source you entered) to other Quowl users in the Community section. You keep ownership of your text. Approved items are shown without your device identifier.

6. Removal on request

You can ask us to remove a submitted or published item at any time by writing to quowl.app@gmail.com. Before a submission is sent (the app keeps it on your device for a short time) you can also cancel it in the app.

7. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest revision.
