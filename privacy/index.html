Privacy Policy

What Quowl collects, why, and how you can reach us about it — in plain terms, covering every source of data the app touches.

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy describes how the Quowl app ("we," "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use it. It applies to every version and language of the app.

1. Information we collect

No account, name, email, or phone number is required to use the app.

Anonymous device identifier

On first launch, the app creates an anonymous ID via Firebase Anonymous Authentication. It isn't linked to your name or email — it exists so the app can technically tell devices apart (for example, when you submit a report).

Data kept only on your device

Favorite quotes and facts, reading history, your own quotes, collections, and settings (language, theme, swipe direction, reminders) live locally on your device and are never sent to our servers, except where noted below (for example, an item you choose to share with the Community).

Reports

Reporting a quote or a fact sends us its ID, category, language, your chosen reason, your anonymous device ID, a timestamp, and — if you write one — a free-text description. Used solely for content moderation.

Community submissions

If you choose to share a quote or fact with the Community, we receive the text, the author or source you optionally entered, the language of the app, your anonymous device ID, and the time of submission. We use this only to moderate and publish Community content. Every submission is reviewed by us before anything is published. Approved items are shown to other Quowl users without your device ID. Until a submission has been sent (a short time after you share it) you can cancel it in the app and it never leaves your device. See the Community Terms.

Analytics & diagnostics

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics collect anonymized usage statistics and crash reports (stack traces, OS version, device model) so we can find and fix bugs, per Firebase's privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Quowl Premium is processed entirely through the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card details — Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. Whether Premium is active is determined on your device from Apple's signed purchase records; no purchase information is sent to us or to any other third party.

2. How we use it

  • Moderating and improving the quote and fact catalog, using submitted reports.
  • Moderating and publishing Community content that you choose to share.
  • Diagnosing crashes and improving app stability.
  • Understanding general usage patterns to guide what we build next.
  • Granting access to premium features once a purchase is verified on your device.

We do not sell and do not share your data with third parties for advertising.

3. Retention & deletion

On-device data is removed automatically when you delete the app. Report data is kept until it's manually reviewed, then deleted. Once a Community submission has been processed (approved or rejected), its record is deleted from our database; we keep a moderation log on our own computer to handle abuse and removal requests. You can ask us to delete your submissions, published or not, by writing to us. To request deletion of data tied to your anonymous ID, write to us — see the contact section below.

4. Children

Quowl isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest revision — continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the update.
