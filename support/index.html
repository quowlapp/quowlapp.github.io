Help with Quowl — Daily Quotes.

Contact us

Questions, bug reports, or feature ideas: quowl.app@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Premium and subscriptions

Restore a purchase

Open Quowl → Settings → Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Quowl, or in Quowl open Premium → Manage Subscription. To avoid being charged for the next period (or after a free trial), cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

Free trial

Where offered, a free trial can be used once per Apple ID for Quowl Premium. It converts to a paid subscription at the price shown before you subscribe unless you cancel in time.

Reporting content

Tap the flag on any quote or fact to report it. Reports are reviewed manually.

Your data

To request deletion of data tied to your anonymous ID, email us. See the Privacy Policy for details.
